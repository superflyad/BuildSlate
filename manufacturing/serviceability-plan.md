# Slate Pocket v1R Serviceability Plan

Status: provisional design requirements; no service procedure or repair validation exists.

## Service priorities

1. Safe battery isolation, removal, quarantine, and replacement without puncture, bending, hidden fasteners, uncontrolled adhesive force, or damage to the display.
2. Non-destructive access to the board, storage where replaceable, radios, speakers, cameras, ports, buttons, and thermal module where architecture permits.
3. Traceable replacement parts, configuration/calibration, secure provisioning, diagnostics, and post-repair functional/safety checks.
4. Seal and thermal-stack restoration using controlled consumables, fixtures, bondlines, compression, and cure.

## Design review checklist

- Entry path, opening direction, tools, temperature/solvent controls, edge protection, and cosmetic damage risk.
- Battery pull/service features, isolation order, connector accessibility, adhesive release, swelling contingency, and damaged-cell handling.
- Fastener visibility, captive/unique hardware, torque access, rework-cycle limits, and galvanic/ground effects.
- Flex/connector access, strain relief, mating cycles, replacement sequence, and mistake-proofing.
- Thermal-interface replacement material, pattern, bondline/contact pressure, contamination control, and verification.
- Display/camera/biometric calibration, radio/antenna tuning impact, secure key handling, data protection, and radio-region control.
- Seal/gasket/adhesive replacement, leak-test access, cure, and proof that claimed ingress performance is restored.

## Repair evidence

For each approved repair, record time, tools, consumables, part/revision, hazards, irreversible steps, failure opportunities, required skills, inspection/test, data/security handling, waste handling, and successful repeated demonstrations. A unit must pass electrical safety, battery/charge controls, thermal sensor checks, core function, configuration/security, and any affected RF/seal tests before release.

## Current blocker

Thin bonded construction, unresolved battery geometry, and unselected adhesives/seals prevent a validated service route. If serviceability cannot coexist with 9.5 mm thickness, ingress intent, or thermal bonding, the decision must be exposed through baseline change control rather than hidden in a destructive repair process.

This plan does not claim repairability, ingress restoration, spare-part availability, repair-network readiness, or field safety.
