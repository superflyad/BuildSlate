# Bills of materials

`slate-v1-bom.csv` is the historical tablet-class Slate v1 concept BOM. Its part strings are placeholders and do not identify selected or purchasable components for Slate Pocket v1R.

The active v1R sourcing roles and authorization state are in [EVT0 BOM strategy](../validation/evt0/bom-strategy.yaml). Supplier selection must follow the [operator workflow](../docs/engineering/operator_workflows.md) and configuration authority before it appears in a released BOM.
