# Battery candidate fit

The [public Enovix AI Platform listing](https://www.enovix.com/product-category/ai-platform) describes the AI1-M01 cell as 80 × 66.74 × 5.44 mm. Its rectangular box occupies 29,045 mm³. Slate Pocket v1R allocates 28,824 mm³ for the **complete pack**, including the cell and all non-cell parts. The published bare-cell box already exceeds that allocation by 221 mm³. The current CAD concept pack envelope is 34,825 mm³, 6,001 mm³ over the allocation. Neither geometry is a selected or qualified pack.

Run `python -m buildslate.battery_fit` to recompute those figures from the manufacturer candidate, profile, source CAD placement, and concept pack envelope. The public [Hardware view](https://superflyad.github.io/BuildSlate/?tab=hardware) publishes the result. The screen checks that the CAD cell dimensions match the source listing and that the target allocation is consistent across inputs.

The Enovix listing's “up to 2C” category is **maximum continuous discharge**. It is not a published charge limit for AI1-M01. The 100 W target divided by the target pack's nominal 23.1 Wh is 4.33 per hour, only a rough power-to-energy pressure indicator. Actual charge current varies with pack voltage, state of charge, and temperature. No 100 W charging claim is supported.

The [November 2026 Enovix Form 10-Q](https://ir.enovix.com/static-files/1557353f-642c-46da-a6d1-751d46e16aeb) says the company stopped system-level AI-1 smartphone field testing with its lead OEM and shifted silicon-anode development toward smaller cells. The public AI1-M01 listing remains useful for a geometry screen, but a custom AI-1 smartphone pack is no longer a preferred sourcing route. That supplier decision does not establish whether Enovix would support a separate Slate program.

Exit requires a custom cell and complete pack drawing that fits the approved allocation or an approved geometry change; tab, protection, connector, insulation, and swelling dimensions; a supplier charge curve; and safety review and measured validation. The 6000 mAh target can fall to the best supplier-validated complete pack under the [approved decision rule](slate-pocket-v1r-decision-baseline.md). This screen does not select a cell or authorize charging.

## Drawing-based 6 Ah pack screen

LiPol Battery's publicly linked [LP4473109 pack drawing and specification](https://www.lipoly-battery.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/LP4473109-3.85V-6000mAh-connector.pdf) describes a 1S1P 6,000 mAh, 3.85 V pack with PCM, JST PHR-2 connector, and no NTC. The nominal body is **110 × 73 × 4.4 mm** (35,332 mm³), with a separate 50 mm lead. The body's maximum drawing dimensions are 111 × 73.5 × 4.7 mm. The nominal body alone exceeds Slate's 28,824 mm³ complete-pack allocation by **6,508 mm³**; it is 28 mm longer and 4.5 mm wider than the current concept tray. The present same-layer space left of the PCB is 89 mm long, 21 mm short of this pack body even before the lead or swelling allowance.

The [editable STEP collision study](../../cad/slate-pocket-v1r/concept-cad/lp4473109-pack-outline.step) places the drawing-derived body at the current tray origin and keeps the rest of the assembly fixed. Exact solid proof finds intersections with the main PCB, rear camera, speaker B, pack tray, and other planning solids. [Inspect the public 3D proof](https://superflyad.github.io/BuildSlate/?model=batteryfit&tab=proof). The body is a conservative box; PCM internals, lead bend, connector, swelling, and tolerances are not modeled. The drawing is linked but not copied into the repository.

The PDF specifies **3 A maximum charge current at 4.2 V maximum charge voltage**, an arithmetic cell-input ceiling of 12.6 W before losses, far below Slate's 100 W wired charging target. It states **6 A maximum continuous discharge**, or 23.1 W at nominal voltage, below Slate's 28 W sustained compute screen before display and radios. Its PCM overcurrent detection band is **2–4.5 A**, internally below the stated 6 A cell discharge limit; supplier clarification is required. The [product page](https://www.lipoly-battery.com/lp4473109-lihv-lipoly-battery-3-85v-high-voltage-6000mah-23-1wh-with-pcm-jst-phr-2/) lists 4.48 V for this MPN while the linked PDF specifies 4.2 V. The screen uses the lower PDF limit. This pack is rejected for the current layout and electrical targets, not selected for Slate.
