# Power safety proof

The v1R has no selected battery pack, released power circuit, or measured charge and discharge result. The [provisional power tree](../../engineering/pcb/power-tree.yaml) is a load estimate. The [auditor](../../buildslate/power_safety_proof.py) defines what a future design release must contain and checks internal consistency. It does not approve battery safety or demonstrate a working phone.

## Controlled release

Place `power/power-safety.json` under `engineering/hardware/released/`. Its `power_safety` record in [hardware readiness](../../engineering/hardware/readiness.yaml) must name the exact JSON, its SHA-256, revision, named reviewer, and approved disposition. The release must use `schema_version: 1`, device `slate-pocket-v1r`, and the same revision and reviewer. A different reviewed file cannot satisfy this gate.

The JSON must bind `selected_bom_sha256`, `electrical_bom_sha256`, and `routed_pcb_sha256` to the exact bytes of `selected-bom.json`, `board.bom.csv`, and `board.kicad_pcb`. The readiness gate also requires approved upstream evidence records for those files and a passing selected-part audit. The selected battery MPN must match the part set. Charger, PMIC, protection, USB PD controller, and USB receptacle component references and MPNs must match the released electrical BOM.

The selected battery's machine-readable capabilities must include minimum and maximum pack voltage, maximum charge current and validated charge power, continuous and peak discharge current, charge temperature window, and maximum operating temperature. These values need traceable supplier evidence in the selected BOM; the auditor does not invent ratings. The power release declares normal charge termination, undervoltage and overvoltage trip points, temperature limits, charge and discharge current limits, charger efficiency, idle system draw, and wired input cap. The checks enforce nested operating and protection thresholds and apply the EVT0 30 W input ceiling. Wireless charging and full power charging during sustained AI load remain disabled for EVT0.

The `usb_input` record also names the receptacle MPN, cable MPN, their declared maximum operating voltages and currents, and the negotiated PD voltage and current. Both rating sources must be actual hashed PDF files under `engineering/hardware/released/power/sources/`; the auditor rejects missing, stale, or outside paths. The PD contract must stay within both hardware ratings and supply the declared wired-input cap. This is source and arithmetic integrity only: a reviewer must verify the PDF values and the implemented controller policy, and EVT0 must measure the physical connection. The [public USB candidate screen](usb_interconnect_fit.md) does not select either component.

Every EVT0 load must appear once: compute, 128 GB memory, 1 TB storage, display, radios, cameras, and system I/O. Compute load cannot fall below the approved 28 W sustained and 45 W peak profile. Each load belongs to exactly one rail. For each rail the auditor checks declared voltage, efficiency, continuous and peak current capacity, and startup dependencies. It converts the summed rail loads through their efficiencies into worst-case pack current at the declared minimum operating voltage. This is a conservative simultaneous-load screen; measured duty cycles and transient waveforms require later work.

The release must declare hardware reference, hardware action, and firmware action for pack overvoltage, undervoltage, overcurrent, overtemperature, charger overcurrent, and USB input overvoltage. The hardware reference must resolve to a selected electrical BOM component. These are design declarations. The auditor cannot establish that a circuit actually trips, that its threshold is safe for the cells, or that firmware responds on a physical unit.

Run `python -m buildslate.power_safety_proof --repo-root .` to inspect the release. The [fault fixture](../../validation/validate_power_safety_proof.py) exercises valid arithmetic and stale or unsafe inputs. The public [Hardware view](https://superflyad.github.io/BuildSlate/?tab=hardware) reports `missing` until a controlled release exists. Even a passing audit only makes the reviewed design evidence eligible for the design-release gate. Supplier approval, protection characterization, thermal and charger measurements, and physical fault tests remain separate requirements.
