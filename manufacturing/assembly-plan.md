# Slate Pocket v1R Provisional Assembly and Rework Plan

Status: process concept; requires released design, supplier work instructions, safety review, fixture trials, and EVT evidence.

## Proposed flow

1. Receive, identify, quarantine/accept, and environmentally control parts/materials by lot and revision.
2. Inspect enclosure/datums, PCBA, display, thermal module, battery, flexes, cameras, acoustic parts, antennas, and cosmetic surfaces.
3. Program and electrically inspect the PCBA under current-limited bench conditions before enclosure installation.
4. Install frame/board locating and grounding features; verify clearances, fastener engagement, and connector access.
5. Apply/inspect thermal interfaces using controlled material lot, pattern, mass or thickness, compression feature, and contact evidence.
6. Install radios/antennas, cameras, speakers/microphones, haptics, buttons, ports, flexes, shields, and strain relief in released order.
7. Dry-fit the supplier-approved battery pack; verify cavity, isolation, protection, pull/service path, and swelling clearance before electrical connection.
8. Install display/rear subassemblies and seals with controlled cleaning, surface preparation, adhesive dispense, pressure, cure, and witness coupons.
9. Perform staged electrical safety/functional tests before irreversible closure and immediately after closure.
10. Complete dimensional, cosmetic, mass, leak/pretest if applicable, RF functional, thermal-sensor, charge-control, and final configuration checks.

## Process controls to define before release

- Station-level tooling/fixture IDs, calibration, interlocks, ESD controls, operator qualification, work instructions, cycle time, and data capture.
- Adhesive/TIM lot, storage, thaw/open time, dispense verification, cure profile, bondline/compression, contamination, and destructive coupon checks.
- Fastener torque-angle or other approved control, sequence, driver trace, thread condition, and rework limit.
- Battery state-of-charge window, transport/storage, handling, isolation, connection order, inspection, emergency response, and quarantine.
- Software/firmware provisioning, keys, secure state, radio region, calibration, serial identity, audit trail, and failed-unit recovery.

## Rework posture

Rework must have an approved instruction, attempt limit, inspection, traceability, and safety review. Battery damage/suspected damage, overheated assemblies, torn high-current interconnects, contaminated safety-critical bonds, or unknown electrical faults are quarantined rather than improvised. Repaired units remain identifiable and are analyzed separately for yield/reliability.

This flow is not a released manufacturing process and does not authorize assembly.
